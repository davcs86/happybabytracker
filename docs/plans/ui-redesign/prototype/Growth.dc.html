What do these numbers mean?
Percentile. Compares one measurement with a reference group of children of the same age and sex. A percentile of 50 is the middle of that group.
z-score. The same comparison counted in steps from the middle. 0 is the middle; each step up or down is one standard deviation.
Corrected age. For a baby born early, this app subtracts the weeks of early birth from age until the second birthday. WHO and CDC do not publish this rule; it is this app’s own choice. Both ages are shown.
This page shows the numbers and does not interpret them. Questions about what they mean for {{ kid }} are for your clinic.
